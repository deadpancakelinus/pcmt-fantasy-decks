Top Trumps

Your top card against theirs. Pick the stat you think wins and take both cards. Hold every card, or the most cards after 60 rounds, to win.

CARDS

How it works

  • Pick NA or EMEA before dealing. A game only ever uses one region's cards: every S3 NA player or every S2 EMEA player, subs included.
  • Each card has five stats: Toxics rating, ACS and KAST straight from the pcmt.gg stats page, plus Mechanics and Brains. The higher number always wins.
  • Whoever has the higher number takes both cards to the bottom of their pile and picks the next stat. A tie puts both cards in the pot, which goes to the next round's winner. With an odd number of players, the spare card starts in the pot.
  • You win by holding every card. If nobody has after round 60 (most games), whoever has more cards wins.
  • Mechanics and Brains marked * are placeholder numbers for now.
