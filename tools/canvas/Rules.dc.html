How to play

Build a deck with 11 PCMT players, stay under budget, and score points every time they play.

Rules

11 Players Two players for each role (duelist, controller, initiator, sentinel) plus three wildcards that take any role. Every player has one role. Every role has to be filled or your deck scores no points.
100 credits Prices run from 4 to 15 credits, based on each player's current leaderboard rating on PCMT.gg. Your eleven can't cost more than 100 credits, so you can't stack every star.
IGL Name one player your IGL. Everything they score counts 1.5×.
2 Teammates No more than two players from any one roster.
Unlimited Transfers Swap as many players as you like while your deck is unlocked. Once it locks for the gameweek, it stays as it is until Monday. IGL swaps are free too: move the IGL tag to anyone in your deck before the lock.
Lock In Your Deck Each gameweek locks 12 hours before its first match (NA or EMEA) on PCMT.gg. No buying, removing, clearing, decks or IGL changes until it reopens at 5:00 AM Monday EDT. If a match is still being played at that point, it reopens once that match ends.

The Toxic rating is the RT column on PCMT.gg. Ties for a Toxic place, top ACS or top KAST: every tied player gets the points. A 13-0 win gets the map win and the +7, not the round-difference points; an overtime win gets the map win only. A 2-1 or 3-2 win gets no series bonus, just the map wins. An ace counts as a 5K only, not also a 4K. Deaths don't cost points.

EXAMPLE

A player gets 20 kills (12 on the first map, 8 on the second), 6 assists, 4 first bloods and 150 ADR, places #2 in Toxic rating, and their team wins the series 2-0 (13-7 and 13-11).

20 kills + 3 for 5-kill marks
+ 3 assists + 2 first bloods + 3 ADR
+ 2 Toxic #2 + 2 map wins
+ 1 won by 6 + 2 for 2-0
= 38 pts
As your IGL: 57 pts

How Credits work

  • Each card in your locked-in deck earns its number from The Deck: that player's points this gameweek.
  • Credits are paid when the gameweek ends, Monday 5:00 AM EDT, as the cards reset to zero.
