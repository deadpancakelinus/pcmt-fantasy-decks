Official Rules

NA Season 4 and EMEA Season 2. Build a deck of 11 PCMT players from one region, stay under budget, and score points every time they play.

Effective November 17, 2026 (Patch Notes 2.0)

§ 1The deck

  1. 1.1Eleven players. Two players for each role (duelist, controller, initiator, sentinel) plus three wildcards that take any role. Every player has one role. Every role has to be filled or your deck scores no points.
  2. 1.2One region. A deck is either an NA deck or an EMEA deck, not both. Because the regions score and play on different schedules, every player in your deck must come from the same region. A deck with players from both regions has to be fixed before it can be locked.
  3. 1.3Two per roster. No more than two players from any one team.
  4. 1.4IGL. Name one player in your deck your IGL. Everything they score counts 1.5×.

§ 2Budget

  1. 2.1100 credits. Prices run from 4 to 15 credits, based on each player's leaderboard rating on PCMT.gg. Your eleven can't cost more than 100 credits.
  2. 2.2Price changes. Prices can change. If your saved deck ends up over 100 credits, it is marked "please relock your deck" and scores no points until you swap players to get it back under budget and lock it again.

§ 3Gameweeks, locks and transfers

  1. 3.1Gameweeks. Gameweeks run Thursday 5:00 AM EDT to the next Thursday.
  2. 3.2Weekly lock. EMEA Decks lock Thursday at 5:00 AM EDT, or 6 hours before the first match if the week's first match is on Saturday or later. They reopen Tuesday at 5:00 AM EDT, or once Tuesday's match result is in if there's a Tuesday match. The deck saved at the lock counts for the whole gameweek; changes after it reopens count from the next gameweek.
  3. 3.3Stage 1 lock. NA NA decks lock on November 21, 2026 and stay locked until the end of Stage 1. The NA deck you have saved at that lock is the one that counts for all of Stage 1, so get it right before then.
  4. 3.4Transfers. Swap as many players as you like, and move the IGL tag freely, while your deck is unlocked. Nothing can be bought, removed, cleared or changed while it is locked.

  1. 4.2Toxic rating. The Toxic rating is the RT column on PCMT.gg. Ties for a Toxic place, top ACS or top KAST: every tied player gets the points.
  2. 4.3Map results. A 13-0 win gets the map win and the +7, not the round-difference points. An overtime win gets the map win only.
  3. 4.4Series results. A 2-1 or 3-2 win gets no series bonus, just the map wins.
  4. 4.5Multi-kills. An ace counts as a 5K only, not also a 4K. Deaths don't cost points.
  5. 4.6Subs. When a player sits out and a sub plays in their place, the sub's points go to the player they replaced, so your card still scores.
EXAMPLE

A player gets 20 kills (12 on the first map, 8 on the second), 6 assists, 4 first bloods and 150 ADR, places #2 in Toxic rating, and their team wins the series 2-0 (13-7 and 13-11).

20 kills + 3 for 5-kill marks
+ 3 assists + 2 first bloods + 3 ADR
+ 2 Toxic #2 + 2 map wins
+ 1 won by 6 + 2 for 2-0
= 38 pts
As your IGL: 57 pts

§ 5NA and EMEA scoring

  1. 5.1EMEA decks. EMEA Every game each player in your deck plays during the gameweek counts toward your score.
  2. 5.2NA decks. NA Only each player's top 2 best games count. Every player in your NA deck scores their two highest-scoring games; the rest of their games don't count toward your deck.
  3. 5.3IGL. In both regions the IGL's counted games score 1.5×.

§ 6Credits

  1. 6.1Earning Credits. Each card in your locked-in deck earns its number from The Deck: the points that player scores for you that gameweek. Cards below zero count as zero.
  2. 6.2Payday. Credits are paid when the gameweek ends, Thursday 5:00 AM EDT, as the cards reset to zero.

§ 7Pick'ems

  1. 7.1NA Season 4. NA Pick the winner of each game before it starts. A right pick earns 10 points; a wrong pick earns nothing.
  2. 7.2EMEA Season 2. EMEA A right pick in the bracket earns 20 points; a wrong pick earns nothing.
