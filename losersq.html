LosersQ

Build a team of 5 from the NA Season 3 player pool, pick a coach, and the bot plays your run through a simulated Season 3: groups, playoffs, every map.

STAFF PREVIEW

How it works

  • Players: every NA S3 player and sub, with their stats from pcmt.gg. Each has an LQ rating (mostly their Toxic rating, plus some ACS and KAST). Players with only a few maps are pulled toward an average rating, so a hot sub on 2 maps isn't a cheat code. No budget yet.
  • Roles: each player starts on their listed role. Tap a role icon under a player on your team to move them; only roles they played in NA S3 can be picked (from the agent they played on every PCMT.gg map). On a new role they play with their numbers on that role: rating, ACS, KAST and deaths. A role they only played a map or two leans toward their overall rating, so one big game doesn't make them a star. Dim icons in the player pool show the other roles each player has played; filtering by a role lists everyone who has played it, with their numbers on it.
  • Team rating: your players' average, minus a bit for a missing controller, initiator, sentinel or duelist (or more than 2 duelists), plus chemistry for every pair who played on the same S3 team.
  • Your spot: your team takes the place of one real team in its group. Players you draft leave their real team, which fills the gap with a sub or a stand-in.
  • The season: NA S3's format. Groups Alpha and Omega, 8 teams each, a Bo3 round robin over 7 weeks. The top 4 of each group go to the same double elimination bracket S3 used (lower final and grand final are Bo5).
  • The bot: plays every series round by round. Teams ban and pick maps, have good and bad maps, good and bad days, and win streaks. Kills, deaths and assists come out of each round played, weighted by each player's stats and role.
  • Coaches: each one changes something different. The Tactician wins map vetoes, the Hype Man rides momentum, the Analyst finds upsets, the Vet shows up in the playoffs, the Bestie gives five randoms real-team chemistry.
  • Every season has a code, and the same code with the same team plays out the same way.
