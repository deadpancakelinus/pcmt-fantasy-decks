Pages that aren't public yet. Players can't see them or open them; you can, with Open. Press Launch (then press again to make sure) to make a page live for everyone: it goes into the menu and the Home tiles and leaves this list. Launching the Store also opens every card pack to everyone.

    Change a price and press Save. The site uses the new price the next time a page loads: The Deck's prices and budget, and the cards in packs. Decks already saved keep counting with the prices from when they were saved, so a price rise never breaks someone's deck; it only applies to new changes. Prices go from 0 to 30 in steps of 0.5.

    Remove takes a player out of The Deck's store straight away: nobody can buy them or get them from a random deal. Managers who already have them keep them in their deck. Put back undoes it.

    Reset

    Every card in packs: its rarity, the pack it comes from, how many copies players own and how many players own it. Retired cards (taken out of packs) are kept for the people who already pulled them.

    Set each pack's price in Credits. While "Open to everyone" is off, only PCMT Team staff and site admins can buy it, so you can test (a price of 0 is free). Odds and what's in each pack come from tools/cards.py.

    Packs

      Series

      Launch a whole series at once: every card in it is released together, into the packs named on its cards. Hold back the series to stop new pulls of all of them.

        Enter each player's 4Ks and 5Ks for a series. 4K +1, 5K (ace) +3 (an ace counts as a 5K only). Everything else comes from PCMT.gg. The new points show on the site the next time a page loads, and Credits pay out with them.

        When a player sits out and someone else plays in their place, enter it here and the sub's points go to the player they replaced: on their card, every deck with them, the Leaderboard and Credits. A sub lasts one gameweek: it counts in that week's series where the sub played for that player's team and the player didn't, and stays active until decks reopen. Nothing carries into later games. You can remove it until the gameweek ends; once that week's points are paid it's locked and moves to Past gameweeks. Changes show on the site the next time a page loads.
