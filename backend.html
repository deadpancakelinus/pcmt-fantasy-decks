Pages that aren't public yet. Players can't see them or open them; you can, with Open. Press Launch (then press again to make sure) to make a page live for everyone: it goes into the menu and the Home tiles and leaves this list. Launching the Store also opens every card pack to everyone.

    Change a price and press Save. The site uses the new price the next time a page loads: The Deck's prices and budget, and the cards in packs. Decks already saved keep counting with the prices from when they were saved, so a price rise never breaks someone's deck; it only applies to new changes. Prices go from 0 to 30 in steps of 0.5.

    Reset

    Every card in packs: its rarity, the pack it comes from, how many copies players own and how many players own it. Retired cards (taken out of packs) are kept for the people who already pulled them.

    Every Rare card. They're held back: packs can't drop a card until it's released here, and a pack's Rare roll gives a Common instead. Release a card and it can be pulled from its pack straight away (the Store shows the new odds on its next load). Hold it back again to stop new pulls; anyone who already pulled it keeps it.

    Set each pack's price in Credits. While "Open to everyone" is off, only PCMT Team staff and site admins can buy it, so you can test (a price of 0 is free). Odds and what's in each pack come from tools/cards.py.

    Everything sent from the "comments and complaints always welcome :)" box on the Home page, newest first. Players can write logged in or logged out (up to 50 words). Press Settled once it's dealt with: that deletes it for good (you'll be asked to press again to make sure).
