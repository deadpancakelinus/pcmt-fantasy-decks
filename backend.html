              Ranked games and RR

              From Riot's ranked history and match stats through HenrikDev (the last ~20 ranked games per account). It checks 15 players every 10 minutes, so each player about every 1.5 hours (the button checks 3 more straight away, and an opened player can be checked on their own); games stay kept here after they drop out of Riot's list. Players need a Riot ID on the Riot IDs page. W–L counts RR won or lost (a loss with derank protection counts as a loss). K/D/A, agent, score and ACS (combat score per round) come from each game's match stats and fill in the next time a player is checked; games without them show —, and a player's K/D/A adds up only the games that have one.
